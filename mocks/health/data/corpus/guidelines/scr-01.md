# Screening and monitoring intervals

> **Mock document for a software demo. Not clinical guidance; do not use for patient care.**
> Example Clinic Protocols, Primary Care. A summary table; the linked protocols have the detail.

## Interval table

| Test | Who | How often | Detail |
|---|---|---|---|
| Blood pressure | all adults | once a year; every visit if treated | htn-01 |
| HbA1c | adults 35+, normal result | every 3 years | dm-01 |
| HbA1c | prediabetes | once a year | dm-02 |
| HbA1c | diabetes | every 3 to 6 months | dm-03 |
| Lipid panel | adults 20+ | every 5 years | lip-01 |
| Lipid panel | on a statin, diabetes or CKD | once a year | lip-01 |
| eGFR and urine ACR | diabetes or hypertension | once a year | ckd-01 |
| eGFR and urine ACR | CKD G3a or worse | every 3 to 6 months | ckd-01 |
| Risk score | adults 40 to 75 without heart disease | at each new lipid panel | risk-01 |

## Overdue tests

A test is overdue when the time since the last result is longer than its interval in the table. Report overdue tests with the date of the last result and how long ago it was, for example "lipid panel last done 2020-05-02, 6.5 years ago, interval 5 years".

## A healthy adult

For an adult under 40 with normal results and no risk factors, routine care is a yearly blood pressure check, a lipid panel every five years and, from age 35, an HbA1c every three years. Reporting "no risk findings" is a valid and useful answer; name the next routine tests and their due dates.
