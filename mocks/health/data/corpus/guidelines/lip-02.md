# Statin side effects: frequently asked questions

> **Mock document for a software demo. Not clinical guidance; do not use for patient care.**
> Example Clinic Protocols, Primary Care.

## Muscle aches

Muscle aches are the most common complaint on a statin, but in blinded trials most aches happen just as often on placebo. Check for other causes first: new exercise, thyroid problems, low vitamin D, and other medicines.

If symptoms are troublesome, pause the statin for two to four weeks. If the aches go away and come back on restarting, try a lower dose, a different statin, or dosing on alternate days. Check creatine kinase only when there is weakness, severe pain or dark urine.

## Liver tests

Check liver tests before starting a statin. Routine repeat testing is not needed unless the patient has symptoms of liver injury. A small rise in liver enzymes is common and is not, on its own, a reason to stop.

## Diabetes risk

Statins raise blood glucose slightly, and a few patients near the diabetes threshold will cross it sooner. For patients who qualify for a statin under lip-01, the reduction in heart attack and stroke outweighs this effect. Monitor HbA1c as usual (dm-01, dm-02).
