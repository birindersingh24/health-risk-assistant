# Type 2 diabetes care and monitoring

> **Mock document for a software demo. Not clinical guidance; do not use for patient care.**
> Example Clinic Protocols, Primary Care.

## Glycemic targets

For most adults with type 2 diabetes, aim for an HbA1c below 7.0%. Relax the target to below 8.0% for older or frail patients, patients with a history of severe hypoglycemia, or patients with limited life expectancy. Write the chosen target in the care plan so that later reviews compare against it.

## How often to test

| Situation | HbA1c | Kidney (eGFR and urine ACR) | Lipids |
|---|---|---|---|
| At target and treatment unchanged | every 6 months | once a year | once a year |
| Above target or treatment changed | every 3 months | once a year | once a year |
| eGFR below 60 | as above | every 6 months, see ckd-01 | once a year |

Add a yearly eye examination and a yearly foot examination.

## Cardiovascular protection

Diabetes adds three points to the cardiovascular risk score (risk-01). All patients with diabetes aged 40 to 75 should be offered at least a moderate-intensity statin, and a high-intensity statin when the risk band is high (lip-01).

The blood pressure target for patients with diabetes is below 130/80 mmHg (htn-02). Prefer an ACE inhibitor or ARB when urine albumin is raised.

## First-line medicine

Metformin is the usual first medicine. Start low, at 500 mg once or twice a day, and increase over several weeks to reduce stomach upset. The usual full dose is 2000 mg a day. Kidney function limits the dose: follow ckd-02 every time eGFR is reported.
