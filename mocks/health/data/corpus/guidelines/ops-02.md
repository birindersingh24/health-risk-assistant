# Recording patient decisions

> **Mock document for a software demo. Not clinical guidance; do not use for patient care.**
> Example Clinic internal policy.

## Shared decisions

Many recommendations in these protocols are offers, not orders. Statins, metformin for prediabetes and the lifestyle program are decided with the patient. A patient may decline after hearing the benefits and risks; that is a valid outcome.

## How to record a decline

Write in the visit note:

- what was offered, such as "moderate-intensity statin",
- the risk information discussed, such as the risk band,
- the patient's decision and their reason if they gave one,
- when it will be raised again.

## A documented decline is not a care gap

Software that looks for care gaps, such as "high risk band and no statin", will still flag a patient who declined, because the decline lives in the notes. Before reporting a gap, check the notes. If a decline is recorded:

- report it as "statin offered and declined on <date>", not as a missed treatment,
- do not recommend offering it again before the planned review date,
- do raise it again early if the risk band has gone up since the decline.

Revisit a declined recommendation at least once a year.
