# Blood pressure measurement and staging (2019)

> **Mock document for a software demo. Not clinical guidance; do not use for patient care.**
> **SUPERSEDED.** This version was replaced by htn-01 in February 2025. Kept for audit history only.

## Taking a reading

Seat the patient and wait five minutes. Take two readings and record the average. Confirm a raised reading at a second visit before diagnosing hypertension.

## Categories (2019)

| Category | Systolic (mmHg) | | Diastolic (mmHg) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Normal | below 120 | and | below 80 |
| Prehypertension | 120 to 139 | or | 80 to 89 |
| Stage 1 hypertension | 140 to 159 | or | 90 to 99 |
| Stage 2 hypertension | 160 or higher | or | 100 or higher |

Under this older scheme a reading of 135/85 was called prehypertension. Under the current htn-01 it is stage 1 hypertension.

## Treatment threshold (2019)

Drug treatment was usually started at 140/90 or higher. The treatment target for most adults was below 140/90. The current target is below 130/80 (htn-02).
